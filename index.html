Your Marvel movies and TV watchlist

Keep your Marvel viewing journey organized with one tracker for MCU movies and series, arranged across Phases One through Six, alongside Marvel titles from Netflix, ABC, Fox, Sony, animation, the Blade trilogy and other releases.

Mark movies as watched, follow TV shows season by season, filter your list by viewing status or Doomsday essentials, and keep an eye on your overall progress. You can also customize posters and back up or restore your tracker data.

Your progress stays in this browser. Use the built-in backup option before clearing browser data or moving to a different website address.

Runtimes are approximate. Shows are tracked by season; hours left counts unchecked titles and seasons. Tap a poster to mark it watched, and the pencil to swap in your own poster. Progress and posters are saved in this browser. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
